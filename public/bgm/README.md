# Background music

Put your loopable Chinese-style BGM here:

- `main.mp3` (required)
- `main.ogg` (optional Safari fallback)

When the file exists, a floating speaker button shows up in the lower-right
corner of the game. Click to toggle play / pause.

## Free sources (CC0 / royalty-free)

- Pixabay Music: https://pixabay.com/music/search/chinese/
- YouTube Audio Library: https://www.youtube.com/audiolibrary
- Free Stock Music: https://www.free-stock-music.com/
- Incompetech (Kevin MacLeod, attribution required)

## AI prompt (Suno / Udio)

> Traditional Chinese palace court music, solo guzheng with soft erhu and
> bamboo flute, calm and reflective, around 90 BPM, two-minute loopable
> section, no vocals, atmosphere of a quiet imperial garden at dusk.

Rename the result to `main.mp3` and drop it in this folder.
